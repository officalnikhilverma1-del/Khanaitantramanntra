TITLE: Character Prompt Bible — Meri Gaon Ki Chudail
PURPOSE: Detailed character prompts for all story characters with strict visual style lock
ASPECT RATIO: 16:9 widescreen ALWAYS

MASTER STYLE BLOCK (APPEND TO EVERY CHARACTER PROMPT)
STYLE: 2D semi-realistic digital illustration. Khooni Monday horror style. Hand-painted look. Thick bold black ink outlines on all elements. Cel-shaded flat color fills with dramatic shadow zones. NO photorealism. NO 3D render. NO anime style. NO watercolor wash. NO sketch look.

COLOR PALETTE — STRICT:
Primary dark: #0D1117 near-black navy
Shadow color: #1A1F2E dark blue-grey
Moonlight: #B8D4E8 cold pale blue-white
Ghost skin: #8BB8C8 cold blue-grey
Living skin: #C17A4A warm wheatish-brown
Blood red: #8B0000 deep blood red — ONLY for eyes and sindoor
Fire orange: #FF6A00 — ONLY for fire scenes and lamp light
Saree white: #E8E8F5 cold off-white for ghost
Clean white: #FAFAF5 warm white for living characters
Ground fog: #2A3545 dark blue-grey mist

LIGHTING RULES — STRICT:
Night scenes: Cold blue-white moonlight from ABOVE.
Deep shadow zones: #0D1117 — hard edge shadows not soft gradient.
Fire scenes: Orange #FF6A00 from BELOW — warm vs cold contrast.
NO flat lighting. NO even ambient light. ALWAYS directional.
Living characters: warm light side + cold shadow side.
Ghost characters: cold moonlight only — no warm light touches them.

OUTLINE RULES:
All characters: thick 3-4px black outline.
Background elements: 2-3px black outline.
Ghost/supernatural: outline slightly thinner + slightly blurred at edges.
Ground fog: NO outline — soft diffuse only.

BACKGROUND CONSISTENCY:
Night scenes BG: #0D1117 sky with #1A2535 clouds.
Ground: #1A1507 dark earth or #1A1A2E dark stone.
Peepal tree: #2A2015 dark brown-grey trunk.
Village houses: #2A1F10 dark warm mud color.
Fog on ground: #2A3545 low-lying blue-grey mist.
Temple/ruins: #1F2535 dark blue-grey stone.

GLOBAL NEGATIVE BLOCK (APPEND TO EVERY PROMPT)
NO photoreal face texture, NO realistic camera grain, NO 3D materials, NO glossy cinematic lens flare, NO anime eyes, NO watercolor bleeding, NO pencil sketch lines, NO extra fingers, NO deformed hands, NO warped text, NO portrait orientation, NO square frame.
