Aap dekh rahe hain… Tantra Mantra.
Yeh kahani gaon ki gawahi, purani yaadon aur chupaye gaye sach par aadharit hai.
Is kahani ka darr kisi bhoot se shuru nahi hota. Yeh darr shuru hota hai ek purane gaon ke jurm se, jise saalon tak chupaya gaya.
Kaaldaas gaon ke bahar ek peepal ka ped hai. Gaon waale kehte hain, wahan aaj bhi Meera naam ki aurat ka dard zinda hai.
Kehte hain jo bhi mard us seema ke andar gaya, 7 din ke andar aag uska faisla kar deti hai.

[SCENE 02 | RAJAN + SHAADI + GALAT MOD]
Yeh kahani hai Rajan ki. 22 saal ka seedha-saadha ladka, jo kheton mein kaam karta hai, maa ka khayal rakhta hai, aur jhagde se door rehta hai.
Ussi raat woh apne dost ki shaadi se laut raha tha. Dhol, nagada, hasna, khana — sab peeche reh gaya, aur raat ke andhere mein usne galat mod le liya.
Woh peepal ke paas pahunch gaya. Rassi dekhkar ruka… phir socha shortcut hai. Jaise hi usne seema paar ki, hawa ka rang hi badal gaya.
Jaise kisi ne us jagah ki saans rok di ho. Tabhi Rajan ne seema ke andar safed kapdon mein ek aurat ko khade dekha.
Chehra saaf dikhte hi Rajan ka khoon jam gaya. Woh mudkar seedha ghar ki taraf bhaaga.
Ghar pahunchkar bhi darr khatam nahi hua. Puri raat usse wahi ladki yaad aati rahi, aur neend aate hi wahi chehra sapne mein saamne khada milta.

[SCENE 03 | PANDITJI TELLS MEERA BACKSTORY]
Agli subah Rajan ki maa Panditji ke paas gayi. Wahin baithkar Panditji ne Meera ki puri kahani sunani shuru ki: saalon pehle is gaon par sukha, bimaari aur bhookh toot padi thi.
Jab jawab nahi milta, bheed wajah nahi… bali ka bakra dhoondti hai.
Us gaon mein rehti thi Meera. Akeli. Jadibootiyon se ilaaj karti thi.
Meera ki alag zindagi ko logon ne daayan hone ka saboot maan liya.
Kisi ke paas saboot nahi tha. Par andhvishwas ko saboot ki zarurat nahi hoti.
Us raat poore gaon ne milkar Meera ko peepal se baandh diya.
Yeh ek aadmi ka paap nahi tha. Yeh sabka faisla tha.
Aag ke beech Meera ne cheekh kam maari… dekha zyada.
Usne kaha — jo bhi mard is ped ke paas aayega, 7 din mein aag uska faisla karegi.
Saat din baad pehli maut mili. Phir doosri. Phir teesri.
Tab se ped band kar diya gaya. Darr se kam… gunaah chhupane ke liye zyada.

[SCENE 04 | POSSESSION + DREAMS + COUNTDOWN]
Rajan ghar toh aa gaya… par us raat ke baad uske khayalon mein wahi safed kapdon wali ladki bas gayi.
Raat ko sapne mein wahi chehra aata. Din mein woh chup rehta. Uski aankhon mein laal pan, awaaz mein bojh, neend mein chalna — sab shuru ho gaya.
Phir din ginne lage — ek, do, teen. Har din uski haalat aur bhaari hoti gayi.
Teesre din Rajan ki maa mandir daudi.
Panditji ne kaha — shaap hai. Phir dheere se bola, main bhi us bheed mein tha.
Shaap todne ke liye mantra kam, sachchi maafi zyada zaroori hai.
Aaj raat barah baje Rajan ko ped ke neeche ghutne tekne honge.

[SCENE 05 | CLIMAX: MAAFI + MUKTI]
Rajan ne sach suna… aur is baar woh darr kar nahi, faisla karke nikla.
Wahi rassi dobara paar hui. Is baar galti se nahi.
Ped ke neeche baithkar usne sindoor aur kala til zameen par rakha.
Meera… tum beqasoor thi. Zulm hamara tha.
Main us waqt nahi tha. Par main usi gaon ka hissa hoon. Main sabki taraf se maafi maangta hoon.
Hawa tez hui. Pattay ghoome. Aur Meera ka roop saamne aa gaya.
Is baar uski aankhon mein badla nahi… thakaan thi.
Hawa mein ek awaaz goonji — Jaao.
Rajan ne aankhein kholi. Andar ki laal aag bujh chuki thi.

[SCENE 06 | ENDING + OUTRO]
Subah gaon ko sach bataya gaya. Kuch log roye, kuch chup rahe.
Saalon purani rassi hata di gayi.
Wahi ped. Wahi zameen. Par iss baar uske neeche darr nahi tha.
Meera chali gayi. Shaap khatam hua.
Kuch aatmaayein badla nahi… sachchi maafi chahti hain. Agle episode mein milte hain.

ELEVENLABS DELIVERY NOTES:
- Read pace: medium-slow, story-led.
- Emphasis words: “Meera”, “7 din”, “maafi”, “Jaao”.
- Keep pauses after ellipses (…) and before reveal lines.
